# Superseded results

**This document is a feature of the project, not an embarrassment.** Every
number below was published, then replaced by a better measurement or a
corrected interpretation. Keeping the trail — old number, what replaced it, and
why — is what lets a reader trust the numbers that remain. Nothing here was
quietly deleted.

Each row: the superseded quantity, where it was published, what supersedes it,
and the reason. Filenames in the older rows are pre-restructure paths; see
`experiments/README.md` for where each report lives now.
